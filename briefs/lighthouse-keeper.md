# The Lighthouse Keeper

## Script
The lighthouse keeper had not seen a ship in eleven years, but every night he lit the lamp anyway.

One evening, a small boat drifted ashore. Inside was a girl holding a map, the lighthouse circled in red.

“My grandfather drew this,” she said. “He told me if I ever got lost, there’d be a light here.”

The keeper looked at the map and recognized his own handwriting.

He smiled, stepped aside, and let her in.
